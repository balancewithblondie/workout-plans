Progressive Overload

How the 90 days are structured
Simple rule — if you complete all prescribed sets at the top of the rep range with good form, move up one machine increment next time.

Example — Lat pulldown 8–12 reps: once you can do 12, 12, 12 with good form, increase the weight and work back up from 8–10 reps.

How to use this

1. Today auto-selects based on your phone's date.
2. Log the weight you use per set — it's saved and shown next time that exercise comes up.
3. Tap "Mark Day Complete" when you finish.
4. Progressive overload: once every set hits the top of its rep range with good form, bump the weight one increment next time.
5. Cardio is optional on strength days if time allows; on recovery days, easy walking is encouraged but not required.
